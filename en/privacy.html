Privacy policy

What data 12 Axes handles and why.

Updated: October 30, 2026

Quiz answers

Answers are used only to compute your result at the moment you submit them. 12 Axes does not store answers in a database or link results to people: the result is encoded in the page address (link).

Payments

Payment is made on Paddle, our Merchant of Record, which handles payment data (name, e-mail, address, payment method) under its own privacy policy. 12 Axes does not receive or store card details.

To unlock the report, the site queries Paddle to confirm the payment and uses a reference to it, without storing them in a database.

Storage in your browser

The site stores preferences (such as language and theme), your last result and, after a purchase, the report access code in your browser. You can delete them in your browser settings.

Analytics

We use traffic analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to measure visits and improve the site. They may use cookies and identifiers.

Hosting

The site and API use infrastructure providers (such as Vercel, Cloudflare and Render) that process requests to deliver content.

Your rights

You can ask for information about personal data handled, correction or deletion, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and similar laws where you live. Ask by e-mail at 12axes.quiz@gmail.com.
